Privacy Policy — Codex Fanpage Publisher

Meta App ID: 1019127357788193 · Effective Date: November 18, 2026 · Contact: stashgosu@gmail.com

Codex Fanpage Publisher is an internal page-management automation tool operated by Anh Vu Nguyen to schedule and publish video Reels, captions, thumbnails, and AI-content disclosures to Facebook Pages and connected Instagram accounts owned and administered within the operator's Meta Business Portfolio.

1. Information We Collect and Process

  • Authorized Meta Graph API Page and Business System User access tokens granted directly by the Page administrator.
  • Page identifiers, titles, captions, video files, and cover images submitted by the Page administrator for publishing.
  • Publishing status, Reel identifiers, and basic engagement readbacks returned by Meta Graph API endpoints.

2. How We Use Information

Processed information is used solely to authenticate with Meta Graph API, upload and schedule Page Reels, attach custom thumbnails, set AI-content disclosures (is_ai_generated), and verify publication status on operator-owned Pages.

3. Data Sharing and Retention

We do not sell, rent, or transfer user data or Meta platform data to unrelated third parties. Access tokens and publishing metadata are stored strictly on the operator's private workstation only as long as needed to run authorized publishing workflows.

Terms of Service

Codex Fanpage Publisher is intended exclusively for authorized administrators of the linked Meta Business Portfolio and Facebook Pages. Users remain responsible for ensuring all published media and disclosures comply with Meta Platform Terms and Community Standards.

User Data Deletion Instructions

Codex Fanpage Publisher does not collect or store external end-user personal profiles. To revoke access or request deletion of any stored authorization tokens or logs:

  1. Open Facebook Settings & Privacy → Settings → Business Integrations and remove Codex Fanpage Publisher, or revoke tokens in Meta Business Settings.
  2. Or email stashgosu@gmail.com with subject Data Deletion Request - 1019127357788193; all locally stored credentials and logs will be deleted within 48 hours.
